ReAngle — re-shoot any video from a new angle

Filmed once, re-shot from a new camera path on a 16 GB GPU. Open-source, built on ComfyUI with LTX-2.5 and open weights.

How it works

  1. Your clipany aspect, 2–10 s
  2. DepthVideo Depth Anything Small lifts each frame into 3D
  3. Camera pathpresets or draw your own in the clip's depth
  4. Warp + fillreproject along the path, fill revealed areas from the background side
  5. LTX-2.5 in ComfyUIIC-LoRA guided re-render in overlapping, re-anchored chunks
  6. ExportMP4, side-by-side, or 9:16 social

Run it yourself

Local GPU (16 GB+)

Runs on your own ComfyUI. Hardware detection picks a safe resolution, and a VRAM watchdog stops a job before your display driver crashes. Tested on an RTX 5080 (16 GB).

Comfy Cloud

No big GPU? Point ReAngle at Comfy Cloud with your Comfy API key. Depth and the video render run on Comfy's GPUs. A short orbit cost about $0.10.
